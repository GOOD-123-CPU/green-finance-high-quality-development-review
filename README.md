# 绿色金融促进高质量发展的经济学机制、研究进展与未来方向

## ——基于中外文献的综述

**A Literature Review on the Economic Mechanisms, Research Progress, and Future Directions of Green Finance Promoting High-Quality Development**

## 📖 论文简介

在全面绿色转型、“双碳”目标与高质量发展并行推进的背景下，绿色金融已由环保融资工具逐步演化为影响资源配置、风险定价与产业升级的重要制度安排。

本文基于中国知网（CNKI）、Web of Science、Scopus 等数据库，对 2016 年以来绿色金融促进高质量发展的代表性中外研究进行系统梳理，并重点讨论：

- 绿色金融缓解融资约束的机制
- 环境与气候风险的资本定价机制
- 绿色创新激励机制
- 产业结构升级
- 绿色全要素生产率
- 绿色金融改革试验区
- 转型金融
- 信息披露与漂绿风险
- 未来研究方向与中国制度建设

## 📝 Abstract

This literature review examines the economic mechanisms through which green finance promotes high-quality development, with particular attention to financing constraints, environmental and climate risk pricing, green innovation, industrial upgrading, and green total factor productivity.

The review also discusses the institutional conditions under which green finance can effectively contribute to economic transformation, as well as emerging issues such as transition finance, sustainability disclosure, and greenwashing.
